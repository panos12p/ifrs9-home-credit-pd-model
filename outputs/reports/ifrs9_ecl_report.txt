Important limitations
---------------------
This is an analytical proxy, not a production IFRS 9 measurement framework.
The model output is treated as a single-period PD and is not a calibrated 12-month or lifetime PD term structure.
Stage 2 uses a fixed PD threshold rather than a relative increase in credit risk since initial recognition.
LGD and EAD are rule-based assumptions because contractual cash flows, recoveries, collateral values, and credit conversion factors are unavailable.
The framework does not include Stage 3, discounting, forward-looking macroeconomic scenarios, or scenario weights.
